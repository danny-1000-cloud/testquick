Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback — message us directly on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.

Message us on WhatsApp

What to include

To help us respond quickly, it's useful to mention:

  • Which test you were using (e.g. webcam, keyboard, gamepad)
  • What device and browser you're on (e.g. iPhone Safari, Windows Chrome)
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen instead

Response time

We aim to reply within 24–48 hours. For bug reports, the more detail you can share, the faster we can track down the issue.

Other ways to reach us

Prefer not to use WhatsApp? You're welcome to leave feedback through the site itself — every test is free to use with no account required, so you can always come back and try again after an update.
