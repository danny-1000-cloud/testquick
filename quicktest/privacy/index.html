Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Testquick is built around one core principle: your camera, microphone, keystrokes, and touch input are yours. We designed every tool on this site to process data locally, in your browser, and nowhere else.

What we don't do

  • We do not record, store, or upload your webcam video
  • We do not record, store, or upload your microphone audio
  • We do not log or transmit your keystrokes
  • We do not track your touch input beyond rendering it on the page you're using
  • We do not require an account, email, or any personal information to use any test

What we do collect

Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic — things like which pages are visited and roughly how many people use the site. This is aggregated, anonymized usage data; it is not tied to your camera, microphone, or keyboard activity in any way.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads based on your browsing activity. You can control ad personalization through your browser settings or by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Browser permissions

Some tests (webcam, microphone) require you to grant browser permission before they can run. You can revoke this permission at any time through your browser's site settings, and doing so will simply stop that specific test from working — nothing else on the site is affected.

Children's privacy

Testquick does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The site itself requires no sign-up or personal data to use.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out via our contact page.
