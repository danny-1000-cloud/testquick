About Testquick

Testquick is a free collection of browser-based device tests — built for anyone who needs a fast, no-nonsense way to check that their hardware actually works.

Whether you're about to hop on a video call, just bought a used laptop, are troubleshooting a controller before game night, or simply want peace of mind before an important meeting, Quicktest gives you a straight answer in seconds: does it work, or doesn't it?

What you can test

  • Webcam — live preview, resolution check, and camera switching
  • Microphone — real-time volume meter and waveform
  • Speakers — left/right channel tones to confirm stereo output
  • Keyboard — visual key-by-key press detection
  • Screen — full-color fullscreen test for dead or stuck pixels
  • Touchscreen — multitouch tracking and accuracy
  • CPS (clicks per second) — timed click-speed test
  • Gamepad — button and analog stick tester for any connected controller

Why it's free, and why it's fast

Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your browser. There's nothing to install, no account to create, and no waiting — the moment you land on a page, the tool is ready. We built it this way on purpose: device testing should be something you can do in the time it takes to read this sentence, not a task that requires downloading software you'll use once.

Built for everyone, everywhere

Testquick works on any modern browser, on desktop or mobile, anywhere in the world — no region restrictions, no language barriers, no paywalls.
